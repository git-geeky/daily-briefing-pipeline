# Animal Science Briefing — Recommended Source Catalog

**This is a FLOOR, not a ceiling.** These sources supplement the general catalog in `sources.md` (the animal edition audits both). They are entry points for daily monitoring — registries, agencies, museums, universities, journals, native-language outlets and licensed photo libraries. Follow leads beyond this list: a press release should be traced to the paper, the dataset, or the agency decision it describes.

**Source appraisal standard:** every row states its practical value and the lens that affects how its output should be weighted (agency, NGO advocacy, zoo/aquarium, industry/trade, state-aligned, university press office, citizen-science, etc.). No row is neutral by default; NGO and press-office material is a pointer to the primary study, not a substitute for it. Rows tagged BOT-BLOCKED were confirmed from search-engine snippets rather than a successful fetch; the live site may differ.

**Languages:** native-language rows are deliberate. Prefer the national-language source for national programmes (a Spanish-language Doñana census, a Japanese prefectural zoo announcement, a Brazilian ICMBio release) and cite the original with a translation of the load-bearing sentence. Per-row language, region, topics and feed URLs live in `animal-sources.meta.json`; liveness evidence was collected on 2026-10-26 and goes stale — the daily source audit is the authority.

**Photographs:** only the licensed-imagery rows (and images on the cited institution's own press-kit pages with explicit reuse terms) may supply article photographs; every embedded image needs a caption with creator, licence, and source URL.
